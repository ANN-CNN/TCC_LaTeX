# Product

This is a **Trabalho de Conclusão de Curso (TCC)** — an undergraduate thesis — written in LaTeX for the **Bacharelado em Engenharia da Computação** program at the **Instituto Federal Fluminense (IFF)**, campus Campos-Centro.

**Title:** Comparativo de Redes Neurais Artificiais para Identificação de Imagens  
**Authors:** Kevin Siqueira Perdomo & Rafael Cruz Ferreira  
**Advisor:** Prof. D.Sc. Fábio Duncan de Souza  
**Institution:** Instituto Federal de Educação, Ciência e Tecnologia Fluminense  
**Expected submission:** October 2025

## Subject Matter

The thesis compares the performance of object detection architectures (YOLO, Faster R-CNN, SSD, EfficientDet, etc.) using standard benchmark datasets. Key evaluation metrics include Precision, Recall, mAP (mean Average Precision), and inference time. The work is framed within computer vision and artificial intelligence.

## Document Standard

The document must comply with **ABNT (Associação Brasileira de Normas Técnicas)** academic formatting rules, enforced via the `abntex2` LaTeX class. The primary language is Brazilian Portuguese (`brazil` babel option). Citations follow the ABNT author-date style (`abntex2cite` with `alf` option).
